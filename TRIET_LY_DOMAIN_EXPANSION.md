This assessment uses only the authorized domain-neutral knowledge files. No domain-specific research or validation was performed. “Ready” means the reusable knowledge can seed a review, not that it is ready for operational decisions in that field.

## Cross-domain transfer boundary

### FACT

- The reusable-asset register identifies evidence labels, authorization, verification, decision states, audit lineage, failure classification, staged change, progressive review, learning, capability graphs, knowledge atomization, and prompt scaffolds as portable patterns.
- It explicitly says to reuse structure, not unvalidated parameters.
- The research backlog says no named domain is claimed as researched in the bounded source set.

### INFERENCE

- All seven fields can begin with the same epistemic and governance scaffolding, but not with identical evidence criteria or decision thresholds.
- Field-specific terminology, metrics, taxonomies, and examples should remain quarantined as `DOMAIN_SPECIFIC` until reviewed and mapped without meaning loss.
- Begin domain expansion with low-consequence, clearly bounded evaluations; defer consequential applications until appropriate evidence and authority exist.

### UNKNOWN

- Whether any listed field has already been evaluated outside this source boundary.
- Which fields have applicable external duties, restricted data, or safety constraints.
- Relative market, educational, legal, research, or operating value of the listed fields.
- Whether transfer improves outcomes compared with established field-native methods.

## Readiness conclusion

### FACT

The authorized corpus contains cross-domain structures but no domain-specific validated case set.

### INFERENCE

The knowledge base is ready to support **domain onboarding and gap review**, not ready to claim expertise or make consequential field decisions. Each domain needs its own evidence, review authority, criteria, and validation record.

### UNKNOWN

Which domain should be prioritized; no comparative domain evidence or approved business priority is available in the allowed source set.
