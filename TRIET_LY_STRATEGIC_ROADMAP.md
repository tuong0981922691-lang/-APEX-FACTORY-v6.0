# Triết Lý — Strategic Roadmap

## Planning boundary

This is a proposed strategy for the **3-, 12-, and 36-month horizons** requested. The periods are planning buckets, not evidence-based delivery estimates. Actions are contingent on authorization, ownership, and the unresolved questions in the permitted knowledge files.

## NOW — first 3 months

### FACT

- The nine seed objects, eight packs, conceptual graph, frameworks, candidate SOPs/prompts, and backlogs are documented.
- Schema, owner, evidence standards, criteria, authority, audit policy, and domain adaptation remain UNKNOWN.

### INFERENCE — proposed priorities

1. Assign an accountable knowledge owner and approve the minimum object/evidence-status conventions.
2. Review seed provenance and confidence without upgrading evidence status.
3. Select a small, authorized, low-consequence representative evaluation set.
4. Test one end-to-end knowledge path: claim → evidence check → human authorization/decision state → audit record → reviewed learning proposal.
5. Define an explicit hold outcome when evidence, authority, or criteria are missing.
6. Keep candidate procedures/prompts inactive until the review and test results are recorded.
7. Record unresolved issues as UNKNOWN and maintain domain-specific quarantine.

### UNKNOWN

Owner identity, permitted evaluation data, acceptable risk, target schema, and whether this work is authorized.

## NEXT — 3 to 12 months

### FACT

The source roadmap calls for owner review, validation of prompts/procedures, tested relationships, verified outcomes, and context-specific criteria.

### INFERENCE — proposed priorities

1. Validate candidate procedures and prompts against approved representative cases.
2. Define and calibrate acceptance criteria, confidence semantics, escalation, override, and retry rules for the selected context.
3. Establish audit retention/access/integrity requirements with privacy and security review.
4. Add only verified outcomes and postmortems; distinguish incidents from modeled risks.
5. Measure retrieval usefulness, consistency of evidence labeling, hold/override rates, and correction quality where appropriate.
6. Expand graph links only with source evidence or explicit INFERENCE labels.
7. Review one additional domain only after its evidence standards and reviewers are named.

### UNKNOWN

Suitable measures, viable validation sample, future authorized domains, and whether workflow metrics are available.

## LATER — 12 to 36 months

### FACT

The source set does not prove automatic learning, runtime graph traversal, autonomous agents, or an operational meta-brain.

### INFERENCE — proposed priorities

1. Reassess capability topology only when runtime evidence and ownership contracts are available.
2. Expand across domains by repeating a governed onboarding/validation process, not by copying thresholds.
3. Compare outcomes over time before claiming that the knowledge system improves decisions.
4. Version frameworks, graph relationships, and knowledge changes with lineage and accountable review.
5. Retire or revise assets that fail validation; retain failure records and evidence state.
6. Consider automation or multi-agent coordination only after authority, boundaries, auditability, and measured benefit are established.

### UNKNOWN

Future architecture, sustainable funding/ownership, long-term comparative value, and whether any automation is appropriate.

## Strategic principles

### FACT

- Evidence discipline, accountable authorization, traceability, risk separation, staged change, and verified learning recur in the authorized files.
- Candidate assets are not approved operating policy.

### INFERENCE

- Sequence investment from epistemic integrity → validation → governed reuse → measured learning → scale.
- Prefer fewer proven knowledge assets to a larger unvalidated catalog.
- A five-year differentiator could be a trusted, auditable learning loop, but its advantage must be measured rather than assumed.

### UNKNOWN

Budget, business objective, governance mandate, adoption rate, and success measures.
