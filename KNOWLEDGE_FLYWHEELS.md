# Knowledge Flywheels

## Scope and notation

Only the five authorized strategy reports were used. A flywheel is a hypothesized reinforcing loop, not proof that the loop currently runs or improves outcomes.

## Candidate loops

### 1. Evidence-to-knowledge loop

Knowledge claim  
↓  
Define claim and criteria  
↓  
Collect/check attributable evidence  
↓  
Accept, revise, reject, or hold  
↓  
Record provenance and rationale  
↓  
Approved knowledge update  
↓  
Better-framed future claims

- **FACT:** Evidence status, validation, distinct outcomes, lineage, and reviewed learning recur in the reports.
- **INFERENCE:** The loop may improve future claim quality if accepted updates are traceable and later evaluated.
- **UNKNOWN:** Whether the loop operates or measurably reduces error.

### 2. Decision-to-outcome learning loop

Knowledge / decision criteria  
↓  
Human-authorized action  
↓  
Observed outcome  
↓  
Audit against intended result  
↓  
Decision review  
↓  
Verified lesson or no update  
↓  
Revised knowledge / criteria

- **FACT:** The strategy documents connect decision, authorization, action, audit, outcome, and verified learning as a proposed path.
- **INFERENCE:** Comparing intended with observed outcomes can expose stale or incomplete criteria.
- **UNKNOWN:** Available outcome data, suitable measures, and causal attribution.

### 3. Failure-prevention loop

Risk model or confirmed incident  
↓  
Evidence-based classification  
↓  
Root-cause review only when supported  
↓  
Corrective proposal  
↓  
Human review and staged change  
↓  
Follow-up evidence  
↓  
Retain, revise, or retire lesson

- **FACT:** The reports require separating incident, modeled condition, forecast risk, and UNKNOWN, and require evidence before causal claims.
- **INFERENCE:** A verified corrective cycle can prevent recurrence and strengthen failure knowledge.
- **UNKNOWN:** Whether there is an incident corpus or evidence of recurrence prevention.

### 4. Audit-completeness loop

Decision/action record  
↓  
Independent reconstruction  
↓  
Find missing provenance, authorization, or result links  
↓  
Record-quality correction  
↓  
More complete future records  
↓  
Easier reconstruction

- **FACT:** Durable audit lineage and independent reconstruction are identified as assets/priorities.
- **INFERENCE:** Better records reduce the cost of later decision and failure review.
- **UNKNOWN:** Audit completeness, independent-review results, or operational audit cadence.

### 5. Retrieval-and-graph loop

Knowledge objects and graph links  
↓  
Authorized retrieval/use  
↓  
Find useful, missing, duplicate, or misleading links  
↓  
Evidence-backed curation proposal  
↓  
Reviewed graph/object update  
↓  
Improved retrieval

- **FACT:** The reports propose measuring retrieval usefulness, preserving canonical objects, and expanding graph links with evidence or explicit inference labels.
- **INFERENCE:** Use feedback can improve discoverability if correction and approval are controlled.
- **UNKNOWN:** Any actual retrieval events, graph traversal, or usability findings.

### 6. Prompt/procedure validation loop

Candidate prompt or procedure  
↓  
Representative authorized case  
↓  
Compare output to explicit criteria  
↓  
Human review and failure capture  
↓  
Revise, approve, or retire candidate  
↓  
More reliable future review

- **FACT:** Candidate prompts/procedures are to be validated before becoming defaults.
- **INFERENCE:** Repeated evaluation can convert a candidate into a context-bounded reusable asset.
- **UNKNOWN:** Validation cases, results, owners, and approval records.

### 7. Domain-onboarding loop

Reusable structure  
↓  
Domain evidence standards and reviewer identified  
↓  
Bounded, representative case review  
↓  
Adaptation gaps and context-specific criteria recorded  
↓  
Approved domain mapping  
↓  
New verified evidence strengthens coverage register

- **FACT:** The domain report says reusable structure may seed onboarding but does not establish validated coverage in named fields.
- **INFERENCE:** Repeating governed onboarding can broaden coverage without pretending thresholds transfer unchanged.
- **UNKNOWN:** Domain priority, authorized cases, and transfer quality.

## Cross-loop safeguards

### FACT

The reports require clear evidence states, accountable review, domain-specific adaptation, and caution against unsupported learning or automation.

### INFERENCE

All loops should stop at hold/UNKNOWN when source, authority, or criteria are insufficient. A loop that rewards volume rather than verified usefulness can amplify low-quality content rather than compound value.

### UNKNOWN

Which loops are feasible, safe, and cost-effective in a selected receiving context.
