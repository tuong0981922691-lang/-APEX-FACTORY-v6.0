# Five-Year Knowledge Vision

## Scope

This is a conditional scenario derived only from the five authorized strategy reports. It is not a forecast, approved investment plan, or statement that the functions exist today.

## One-year horizon — trusted foundation

### FACT

The current corpus has domain-neutral seeds and frameworks, but ownership, target criteria, validated procedures, operational evidence, and domain coverage remain open.

### INFERENCE

In one year, with an owner and authorization, the knowledge system could become a **reviewable, auditable knowledge service**:

- Canonical objects retain source, evidence status, confidence rationale, and relationships.
- A bounded set of candidate review methods has been evaluated on authorized representative cases.
- Decision outcomes, holds, corrections, and rationale can be reconstructed.
- At least one context has explicit evidence standards and reviewers.
- Knowledge updates remain human-reviewed; no claim of autonomous adaptation is required.

Potentially supported fields are those selected and validated by authorized experts. The present evidence alone cannot declare any field operationally ready.

### UNKNOWN

Whether an owner, authorized cases, or a receiving context will be available within one year.

## Three-year horizon — governed cross-context capability

### FACT

The reports identify domain onboarding, separate evidence standards, tested relationships, verified outcomes, and measured retrieval/usefulness as possible later work.

### INFERENCE

By year three, successful validation could support a **governed knowledge network** able to:

- Reuse epistemic and audit structures across multiple contexts.
- Maintain separate domain criteria and reviewers rather than copying thresholds.
- Link claims, evidence, decisions, outcomes, corrections, and source lineage.
- Detect knowledge gaps and candidate contradictions for human review.
- Compare asset usefulness and retire or revise weak materials.
- Support research, education, business, creative review, or other selected fields only where domain evidence and authority are established.

### UNKNOWN

Number of contexts, interoperability, retrieval performance, staffing, cost, and comparative outcomes.

## Five-year horizon — evidence-compounding learning ecosystem

### FACT

The strategy reports describe a trusted, auditable learning loop as a potential differentiator while explicitly noting that its advantage is unproven.

### INFERENCE

If the earlier conditions are met, the system could become an **evidence-compounding knowledge ecosystem** that:

- Maintains long-lived, versioned source-to-decision-to-outcome lineage.
- Offers domain-bounded knowledge with explicit confidence, unresolved questions, and review status.
- Learns from verified outcomes through accountable change control.
- Reuses validated methods while preserving local authority and criteria.
- Helps users compare evidence, reconstruct decisions, and find gaps without overstating certainty.
- Incorporates automation only for validated, bounded tasks with human authorization and audit safeguards.

Its likely value would come from accumulated trusted evidence and correction history—not sheer content volume, named “brains,” or automation by itself.

### UNKNOWN

Whether such a system is technically, economically, legally, or organizationally feasible; whether it will serve any particular field; whether the evidence base will compound; and whether outcomes will be superior to alternatives.

## Investment choices

### FACT

No comparative business priorities, budget, owners, or success metrics are established in the permitted reports.

### INFERENCE

- **What creates the most growth:** a repeatable evidence → decision → observed outcome → reviewed learning cycle, if it produces measurable improvements.
- **What creates the strongest potential advantage:** trusted provenance and a multi-year history of validated decisions and corrections.
- **What deserves the most investment:** ownership, domain-appropriate validation, auditability, and measured learning before scale.
- **What to stop or defer:** unvalidated content-volume expansion, framework proliferation, claims of autonomous brains, and automation/multi-agent buildout without demonstrated need and governed benefit.

### UNKNOWN

The actual growth mechanism, user demand, advantage, investment level, and opportunity cost of stopping or deferring any initiative.
