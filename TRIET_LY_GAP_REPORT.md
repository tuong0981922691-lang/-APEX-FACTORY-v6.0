# Triết Lý — Knowledge Gap Report

## Scope and rating meaning

This assessment uses only `KNOWLEDGE_SEEDS.md`, `KNOWLEDGE_PACKS.md`, `MASTER_KNOWLEDGE_GRAPH.md`, `REUSABLE_ASSETS.md`, `KNOWLEDGE_ROADMAP.md`, `KNOWLEDGE_OBJECT_INDEX.md`, `FRAMEWORK_INDEX.md`, `RESEARCH_BACKLOG.md`, and `ACTION_BACKLOG.md`. Ratings describe the documented knowledge set, not a deployed or validated system.

- **Strong:** explicit, repeated, and linked across multiple authorized artifacts.
- **Medium:** a recognizable framework exists, but execution, thresholds, authority, or validation is unresolved.
- **Weak:** only a limited or inferred representation exists.
- **Missing:** no adequate representation or evidence appears in the permitted corpus.

## Final questions

### FACT

- **Strongest:** epistemic discipline, verification concepts, authorization gates, and separation of actual events from risks.
- **Weakest:** validated operating criteria, accountable ownership, and destination-specific evidence.
- **Three strategic assets:** (1) evidence-status and provenance discipline, (2) human-gated decision states, (3) traceable audit-to-verified-learning chain.

### INFERENCE

- **Next investment:** review and validate a small number of linked workflows against authorized representative cases, recording outcomes and gaps. Do not expand domain coverage by relabeling generic principles alone.
- **Largest five-year differentiator:** a continuously governed evidence-and-learning loop that can show provenance, explain decisions, detect unsupported claims, and update only through accountable review. This is a strategic hypothesis, not a demonstrated result.

### UNKNOWN

Whether this investment will produce a measurable advantage, which measures would establish that, and whether future use cases permit the same controls.
