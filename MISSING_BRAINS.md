# Missing and Weak Cognitive Functions

## Important distinction

“Brain” here is shorthand for a cognitive function, not a claim of a separate autonomous agent. Ratings describe conceptual definition and evidence in the five authorized reports; they do not establish deployment.

## Function assessment

| Function (“brain”) | Assessment | Strong / weak / missing |
|---|---|---|
| Survey | **READY TO SPECIFY** | **Strongest:** broad-to-specific review is a recognizable function. Weak in source coverage, completion criteria, and evidence standards. |
| Analysis | **READY TO SPECIFY** | **Strong:** claim, criteria, provenance, checks, and comparison are represented. Weak in domain calibration and conflict resolution. |
| Critic | **PARTIAL** | Independent checks and rejection/revision appear. Weak in independence guarantees, ownership, and performance evidence. |
| Decision | **READY TO SPECIFY** | Human gate and four outcome states are clear. Weak in authority hierarchy, thresholds, exceptions, and transitions. |
| Audit | **PARTIAL** | Traceability is a repeated principle. Weak in operational lifecycle, access/retention/integrity rules, and demonstrated reconstruction. |
| Learning | **PARTIAL** | Verified learning is described. Weak in update review, measured impact, and evidence that knowledge actually adapts. |
| Strategy | **PARTIAL** | Staging and risk-aware prioritization provide a basic strategic scaffold. Weak in objectives, trade-off method, resource allocation, and long-term validation. |

## Which are absent?

### FACT

- The readiness report assigns Survey, Analysis, and Decision READY at the conceptual review/specification level; Critic, Audit, Learning, and Strategy PARTIAL.
- No function is rated MISSING in that capability-level readiness table.
- No fixed autonomous-brain count, actual runtime topology, independent meta-brain, or deployed function is established.

### INFERENCE

- **No listed cognitive function is completely absent from the documented conceptual model.** The most deficient are Critic, Audit, Learning, and Strategy because they lack independent contracts, operational criteria, validation, or measured outcomes.
- **Operationally confirmed brains: none established by this evidence set.** That is an evidence limitation, not proof that such functions do not exist.
- The most important missing capability is not another named brain but a governed end-to-end feedback mechanism with explicit ownership, evidence, decision transitions, and verified learning.

### UNKNOWN

- Whether functions are performed by people, tools, agents, or combinations.
- Whether critics are genuinely independent of proposal authors or decision owners.
- Whether strategy and learning are distinct activities or conceptual groupings.
- Whether a coordinating/meta-evaluative function exists in practice.

## Evidence needed to strengthen functions

### FACT

The source reports identify missing owners, I/O contracts, validation records, update history, representative cases, and measured outcomes.

### INFERENCE

For each function, require a named purpose, accountable owner, inputs/outputs, handoff conditions, acceptance criteria, evidence trail, exception handling, and validated performance before calling it operationally ready.

### UNKNOWN

Who can approve those contracts and what quantitative readiness bar should apply.
