## Domain coverage backlog

### FACT

The seed inputs require retaining assets that can transfer across future ecosystems and tagging domain-bound content, but they provide no researched domain-by-domain evidence set.

### INFERENCE

Maintain a coverage register before asserting a domain is researched. A domain should be “covered” only after an authorized, evidence-backed review and target-specific validation.

### UNKNOWN

The scope, owner, and order of domain research are not defined. No domain is claimed as researched by this backlog.
