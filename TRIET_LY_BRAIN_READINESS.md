# Triết Lý — Cognitive Function Readiness

## Rating definition

Ratings describe the readiness of each **documented capability model** for review, not the existence or production readiness of an autonomous brain:

- **READY:** function and its minimum purpose/position are explicit enough to identify as a reusable capability.
- **PARTIAL:** a function is suggested, but a material input, authority, transition, validation, or operating rule is unresolved.
- **MISSING:** the authorized knowledge set does not establish the function as a distinct capability.

No rating proves deployment, independent agency, or effectiveness.

| Function | Rating | Evidence in authorized knowledge | Main gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Survey | **READY** | Broad scan followed by risk identification appears in progressive evidence review. | Scope, source coverage, and survey completion criteria are not calibrated. |
| Analysis | **READY** | Claims, criteria, provenance, checks, and evidence comparison are represented. | Target-specific criteria, baselines, and conflict rules are unresolved. |
| Critic | **PARTIAL** | Independent checks, evaluation, and rejection/revision paths are described. | Separate critic ownership, independence standard, and evaluator performance are UNKNOWN. |
| Decision | **READY** | Human authorization and accept/revise/reject/hold outcomes are explicit. | Authority hierarchy, exceptions, transition rules, and thresholds are unresolved. |
| Audit | **PARTIAL** | Durable, append-only traceability is a repeated principle. | Retention, access, integrity, completeness, and operational audit process are not specified. |
| Learning | **PARTIAL** | Verified learning and evidence-backed knowledge updates are described. | Update review, measurement, ownership, and adaptive behavior are unverified. |
| Strategy | **PARTIAL** | Staged/reversible change and risk-proportional review provide strategic controls. | No target-specific strategy, resource allocation, or validated long-horizon decision method is supplied. |

## Interpretation

### FACT

- The conceptual pipeline names intake, framing, proposal, evaluation, decision, action, audit, and learning capabilities.
- The source set does not establish a fixed number of autonomous agents, an operating meta-brain, or actual runtime topology.
- Frameworks and candidate procedures/prompts are not evidence of an implemented capability.

### INFERENCE

- Survey, analysis, and decision have the clearest reusable conceptual definitions; critic, audit, learning, and strategy need stronger operational contracts.
- Treat readiness as “ready to specify/review,” never “ready to deploy,” until authorized validation evidence exists.
- A function can be present in a knowledge model without being a distinct brain, service, team, or agent.

### UNKNOWN

- Which functions currently exist in actual operation.
- Whether functions are human, automated, shared, or independently agentic.
- Capability quality, reliability, throughput, and outcomes.
- Whether coordination or meta-evaluation is performed by a distinct entity.

## Minimum evidence to upgrade a rating

### FACT

The authorized backlog already identifies missing ownership, input/output contracts, validation records, update history, and measured outcomes.

### INFERENCE

For any function, an upgrade from partial conceptual readiness to operational readiness should require an accountable owner, explicit inputs/outputs, acceptance criteria, representative validation, recorded exceptions, and auditable outcomes.

### UNKNOWN

The receiving context's authority to collect that evidence and the exact acceptance thresholds.
