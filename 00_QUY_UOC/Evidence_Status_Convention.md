# Evidence Status Convention

## FACT

- **FACT:** Directly stated in a cited source. A citation or source reference is required.
- **INFERENCE:** A reasoned generalization or interpretation; state its supporting facts and reasoning.
- **UNKNOWN:** Not established by available evidence, conflicting, or outside the authorized scope.

## Confidence

Confidence labels indicate source support, not probability or operational effectiveness:

- **High:** explicit and reinforced by multiple source records.
- **Medium:** supported, but a material part is inferred or candidate.
- **Low:** mainly a proposed generalization and requires review.

## Rules

- Do not promote inference or absence of evidence into fact.
- Do not treat UNKNOWN as pass or fail.
- Keep confirmed incidents distinct from modeled conditions and forecast risks.
- Preserve source lineage when summarizing or migrating.
