# KNOWLEDGE_PACKS

## Pack design

Packs group reusable seeds for retrieval. Membership means topical relevance, not operational approval. Seeds and packs are derived only from the five permitted knowledge artifacts.

## 1. Decision Making

- **Seeds:** KS-001, KS-002, KS-003, KS-007, KS-009
- **Purpose:** Frame a decision with explicit evidence status, evaluation criteria, accountable authorization, and distinct outcomes.
- **FACT:** Decision and gate structures are documented in the source artifacts.
- **INFERENCE:** This pack can support future decision records in varied fields.
- **UNKNOWN:** Domain-specific criteria, risk limits, and authority assignments.

## 2. Audit

- **Seeds:** KS-001, KS-004, KS-005
- **Purpose:** Preserve provenance and reconstruct how an outcome was reached while distinguishing observed events from risks and hypotheses.
- **FACT:** Durable audit and incident/risk separation are explicit themes.
- **INFERENCE:** The linked lifecycle record is a reusable audit model.
- **UNKNOWN:** Retention, access, integrity, and regulatory requirements.

## 3. Learning

- **Seeds:** KS-001, KS-004, KS-005, KS-008
- **Purpose:** Derive transferable lessons from traceable outcomes and verified explanations.
- **FACT:** Learning is conditioned on evidence and root-cause verification in the source knowledge.
- **INFERENCE:** Versioned review can prevent unsupported findings from changing future criteria.
- **UNKNOWN:** Whether learning is automatic and how improvement is measured.

## 4. Failure Analysis

- **Seeds:** KS-001, KS-003, KS-005, KS-008
- **Purpose:** Separate confirmed incidents, modeled error paths, forecast risks, and unknowns; investigate in proportion to impact.
- **FACT:** The source artifacts explicitly warn against claiming undocumented incidents or causes.
- **INFERENCE:** Use the categories as separate record types.
- **UNKNOWN:** Actual incident history outside the source set and target severity rubric.

## 5. Knowledge Extraction

- **Seeds:** KS-001, KS-003, KS-004, KS-008
- **Purpose:** Atomize claims, preserve evidence lineage, normalize cautiously, and hold unsupported content for review.
- **FACT:** The migration source calls for atom fields, provenance, evidence status, and cross-links.
- **INFERENCE:** Extraction should preserve uncertainty and original meaning while abstracting context.
- **UNKNOWN:** Target granularity, vocabulary, and import schema.

## 6. Human Approval

- **Seeds:** KS-002, KS-003, KS-007, KS-009
- **Purpose:** Keep consequential authorization with an accountable human after validation and before action.
- **FACT:** Human authorization is documented as a gate.
- **INFERENCE:** Approval rigor should scale with impact and difficulty of reversal.
- **UNKNOWN:** Role definitions, exception paths, and sign-off requirements.

## 7. Governance

- **Seeds:** KS-001, KS-002, KS-004, KS-005, KS-007, KS-009
- **Purpose:** Keep epistemic status, authority, audit, risk distinction, staged change, and outcome states consistent.
- **FACT:** These controls recur across the supplied frameworks and migration guidance.
- **INFERENCE:** A governance layer can define minimum record and gate requirements.
- **UNKNOWN:** Who owns governance in the receiving ecosystem and how policy conflicts are resolved.

## 8. Multi-Agent Coordination

- **Seeds:** KS-003, KS-004, KS-006, KS-009
- **Purpose:** Coordinate capability stages and information handoffs without presuming that separate agents exist.
- **FACT:** The sources describe linked functions, evaluators, audit responsibilities, and a pipeline; they do not prove a fixed count of autonomous agents or a coordinator brain.
- **INFERENCE:** If multiple agents are introduced later, use explicit inputs/outputs, dependencies, evidence handoffs, outcome states, and human authorization gates.
- **UNKNOWN:** Whether a multi-agent system exists, how agents are assigned, and who resolves disagreement.
- **Status:** **Coordination framework candidate; agent existence unverified.**

## Cross-pack links

**FACT:** The same seed may support multiple knowledge tasks.  
**INFERENCE:** Keep one canonical seed ID and reference it from multiple packs to prevent contradictory duplicate copies.  
**UNKNOWN:** Target system support for shared references and pack versioning.
